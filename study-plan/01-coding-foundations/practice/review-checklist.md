# Review Checklist

For every problem, record yes/no:

- I clarified constraints before selecting an approach.
- I described a brute-force baseline.
- I stated the invariant in one sentence.
- I traced a normal example and a boundary example.
- My Java code compiles without IDE repair.
- I tested empty, singleton, duplicates and extreme values where applicable.
- I stated time and auxiliary space with the correct variables.
- I explained why the chosen pattern is valid and when it fails.
- I could reproduce the solution after 24 hours without memorizing lines.

Readiness target: two consecutive 45-minute sessions with one medium solved correctly in ≤35 minutes and explained cleanly in the remaining 10.
