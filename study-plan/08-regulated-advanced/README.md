# Parent 08 — Regulated and Advanced Systems

This parent applies the earlier backend, platform and ML foundations to high-impact
data, specialized accelerator serving and shared-platform economics.

No security/privacy/compliance, healthcare/fintech, accelerator-hardware, or SaaS economics knowledge is assumed.

## Phase A — Prerequisites

1. [Security Foundations](05-security-foundations/lesson.md) — assets, threats, CIA, identity, authentication, authorization, cryptography, application/cloud security, vulnerabilities and incident basics.
2. [Privacy, Audit, Risk, and Compliance Foundations](06-privacy-compliance-foundations/lesson.md) — personal data, lifecycle, purpose/minimization, consent, rights, audit evidence, controls, risk and healthcare/fintech regulatory vocabulary.
3. [Healthcare and Fintech Domain Foundations](07-healthcare-fintech-domain-foundations/lesson.md) — care/payment actors, clinical/claims data, FHIR basics, money, ledgers, payment rails, reconciliation and domain safety.
4. [Computer Hardware and Accelerator Foundations](08-hardware-accelerator-foundations/lesson.md) — CPU/memory/storage, instruction/vector/parallel execution, GPU architecture, precision, bandwidth and performance arithmetic.
5. [SaaS Multitenancy and Cloud Cost Foundations](09-saas-multitenancy-cost-foundations/lesson.md) — tenant lifecycle/isolation, identity/data/config/quotas, metering, pricing, allocation, unit economics and cost governance.

## Phase B — Existing advanced sequence

6. [Security, Privacy and Audit](01-security-privacy-audit/lesson.md) — complete; includes identity/authorization, tenant isolation, cryptography/key lifecycle, privacy/data handling, supply chain, incident evidence and six passing audit-chain tests.
7. [Healthcare and Fintech Design](02-healthcare-fintech-design/lesson.md) — complete; includes exact money/ledger, idempotency/sagas/reconciliation, FHIR/consent/provenance, human oversight and eight passing decision-policy tests.
8. [GPU Inference](03-gpu-inference/lesson.md) — complete; includes CUDA execution/memory, KV-cache arithmetic, batching/quantization/parallelism, scientific benchmarking and seven passing capacity tests.
9. [Multitenancy and FinOps](04-multitenancy-finops/lesson.md) — complete; includes isolation/stamps, data/noisy-neighbor/fairness controls, allocation/unit economics/forecasting and six passing exact-allocation tests.

After all nine lessons, complete the [Regulated and Advanced Systems Capstone](CAPSTONE.md), including its mandatory isolation, ledger, FHIR, GPU, overload, allocation and incident experiments.
